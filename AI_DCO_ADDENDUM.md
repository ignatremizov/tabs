# AI and the DCO

The Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) applies to AI-assisted contributions.
By signing off, you certify that:

- You reviewed and tested the code you submit.
- You have the right to submit the code under this project's license.
- Any AI assistance does not change your responsibility for correctness and
  licensing.

This addendum does not modify the DCO itself; it clarifies how the DCO applies
to AI-assisted work.
